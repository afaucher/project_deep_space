# Recovery Mining Operations

With the spatial mechanics of the Foam now physically manifesting as "currents" that push boundary-breachers towards the North and South poles of each bubble, a new gameplay and economic loop emerges: **Recovery Mining**.

## The Mechanism

Any ship, asteroid, or piece of debris that wanders past the habitable boundary of the bubble is subjected to the Foam's forces. These spatial currents relentlessly drag matter deeper in and laterally towards the poles. 
Eventually, anything caught in this flow is violently displaced (teleported) and spat back out near the extreme ends of the pocket space (the poles). 

Over time, the poles become massive dumping grounds. Dead ships, lost cargo, and dense mineral-rich asteroids naturally congregate in these regions.

## Recovery Mining Outposts

The most dangerous, yet profitable, industry in the Sovereign Drift is Recovery Mining. Specialized outposts are anchored as close to the poles as physically safe. 
Tugs and heavily armored mining ships make short, perilous runs into the churning edge of the foam to drag out wrecks and asteroids before they are violently displaced again or before the current crushes them.

**Gameplay Features:**
- **Dynamic Resource Generation**: Asteroids and ship hulks naturally spawn near the poles, providing an endless, lore-friendly source of salvage.
- **Environmental Hazard**: Ships attempting to scavenge the poles must contend with the chaotic currents. If they drift too far out while towing a heavy wreck, they risk being dragged past the threshold and teleported (or destroyed).
- **Specialized Towing Gear**: This necessitates the development of tractor beams or grappling cables that allow ships to fish out salvage from the safe side of the boundary.
