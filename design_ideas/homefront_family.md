# The Homefront Family (canon, decided 2026-07)

The family shape behind the M39–M44 "Check on Todd" arc. Settled in-session
while authoring Todd's conversation; recorded so Stephanie's payoff dialogue
and any future family content stay consistent.

## The shape

- **Aunt Stephanie is aunt to BOTH the player and Todd.** Not Todd's mother
  — her own line "tell that boy his aunt says to CALL"
  (aunt_stephanie.dialogue) is the canon anchor. The player and Todd are
  cousins, kids of different siblings in her family.
- **Stephanie is the family anchor.** She runs the repair dock at Ironhold
  and the scattered family orbits her. "Family doesn't pay" is not a
  discount — it's her worldview: the whole extended family is her
  responsibility.
- **Todd's parents are out of the picture** (off-cluster, or simply not
  around — deliberately unspecified). Stephanie is the closest thing he has
  to a parent nearby. That is WHY three weeks of his silence lands on *her*
  specifically, and why her demand that he call carries weight beyond
  nagging.
- **The player and Todd grew up together around Steph's dock.** He's the
  cousin who moved out to the Drift to do his own thing — independent,
  flaky about staying in touch ("even for him that's a new record"), but
  not estranged. No ice to break between the cousins; just ribbing.

## Dialogue implications

- Todd says **"Aunt Steph"**, never "Steph" or "Mom" — one word telegraphs
  the shared relationship.
- Todd recognizes the player by **voice, not ship** ("I know that voice") —
  the player's hull and ship name are not fixed (ship names are randomized
  per launch), so any "I know that ship" line is fiction-fragile.
- Todd ordered his replacement antenna **through Prell** (Rock Bottom), who
  told the player they'd never heard of him. That's not a plot hole — it's
  Prell. Todd's take: "And THAT is why I like Prell." Anonymity as
  neighborliness; the Drift's social contract in one line.
- **The present** (quest item `stephanies_present`): UNDISCLOSED until the
  delivery — Todd hands it over sealed ("don't shake it, don't scan it —
  she opens it first"), so the reveal belongs to Stephanie's payoff
  conversation (M44 part 2, co-authored). What's established: it's
  something she once mentioned wanting and said was impossible to find,
  and Todd found it in the guts of the third mining rig his crew stripped
  for parts — tying his three weeks of double shifts (the reason he never
  called) directly to the gift.
